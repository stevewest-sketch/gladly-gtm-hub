Mastering Answers

Answers are the knowledge base that powers Sidekick's responses. High-quality Answers lead to accurate, helpful AI conversations.

  • Public Answers: Visible to customers via AI
  • Internal Answers: Agent-only reference material
  • Best Practice: Keep answers concise and scannable
  • Tip: Use consistent formatting across all answers

Try It Now

1

Navigate to Sidekick > Answers in your Gladly instance

2

Click "Create Answer" and choose a common FAQ topic

3

Write a clear, concise answer (2-3 sentences)

4

Set visibility to "Public" and save
